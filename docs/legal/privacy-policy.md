# Stickreate — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

Stickreate is a sticker-making app. It runs entirely on your iPhone.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Stickreate does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## How your media is handled

- Photos, videos, and GIFs you choose are processed **on your device** to create stickers.
- Your stickers, packs, and the original media you imported are stored **only on your device**
  (in the app's own storage). They are never uploaded anywhere.
- The app makes **no network requests**.

## Permissions

- **Camera** (optional): used only to take a photo you want to turn into a sticker. The image stays on your device. You can decline; the app still works with your photo library.
- **Photo library**: accessed through the system picker only for the items you select; Stickreate does not request full library access.
- **Pasteboard**: when you choose "Add to WhatsApp", the app writes a sticker pack to the system pasteboard so WhatsApp can read it. A locally-built file is written only when you explicitly export one.
- **Files**: only when you choose to import/export a pack file.

## Children's privacy

Stickreate does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Your content and third-party rights

You are responsible for the media you import. Do not use images or videos you do not have the
right to use. Stickreate and WhatsApp are not affiliated; "WhatsApp" is referenced only to
describe a compatibility action.
