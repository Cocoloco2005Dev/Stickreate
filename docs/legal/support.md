# Stickreate — Support

Stickreate turns your photos, videos, and GIFs into WhatsApp sticker packs, entirely on your iPhone.

## Getting started

1. **Create a pack** — open the Library and tap **+**.
2. **Add stickers** — pick photos/videos/GIFs (or take a photo). Edit each with **Intelligent Cut**, manual cutout tools, and crop; videos can be trimmed, cropped, and cut.
3. **Add to WhatsApp** — once a pack has **3–30** stickers, tap **Add to WhatsApp** and confirm in WhatsApp's preview.

## Frequently asked

**A pack needs at least 3 stickers.** WhatsApp requires 3–30 per pack, and a pack can't mix
static and animated stickers. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a Stickreate one.

**"Add to WhatsApp" shows an error (error 1000), but it works the second time.**
This is a known WhatsApp timing quirk. Stickreate waits briefly between writing the pack and
opening WhatsApp. If it happens, just try again.

**I re-imported a pack and now there are two.** Re-importing a pack with the same content creates
a duplicate — a long-standing WhatsApp bug on iOS (there's no "update" for imported packs). Delete
the old one first, or use a new pack.

**Can I send a pack to a friend?** Not from an app on iOS — WhatsApp doesn't provide a public way
for third-party apps to share a pack to another person. You can export a `.wasticker` file and
share it as a file instead.

**Does the app need internet or an account?** No. Everything is on-device: no accounts, no
network, no analytics.

**How do I save space?** Settings → **Clear Cache**, and turn off **Keep original sources** to stop
storing the original media for re-editing.
